# Information Security Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to establish a framework for protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the company's information assets.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who have access to the company's systems and data.

## 3. Security Governance
- The CTO is responsible for the oversight of the security program.
- Security reviews are conducted quarterly.

## 4. Risk Management
- A formal risk assessment is performed annually to identify and mitigate threats to the system.

## 5. Asset Management
- All hardware and software assets are tracked in a centralized inventory.
- Assets must be classified based on sensitivity (e.g., Public, Internal, Confidential).

## 6. Access Control
- Access is granted based on the principle of least privilege.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is required for all administrative access.
- User access is reviewed every 90 days.

## 7. Change Management
- All changes to production systems must be documented and approved via pull requests.
- Changes must be tested in a staging environment before deployment.

## 8. Incident Management
- All security incidents must be reported immediately to the Security Engineer.
- The company follows the documented Incident Response Plan.

## 9. Compliance
- Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action.
