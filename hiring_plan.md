# Hiring Plan for Founding Engineer (CTO)

## Role Overview
We are seeking a founding engineer who will serve as our Chief Technology Officer (CTO). This is a critical technical leadership position responsible for setting the technical vision, architecture, and execution strategy for our subcontractor compliance platform.

## Primary Responsibilities
- Own technical roadmap, architecture, staffing, and execution
- Build and lead the engineering team
- Define and implement the core product architecture
- Make key technology decisions (stack, frameworks, infrastructure)
- Ensure technical excellence and delivery quality
- Collaborate with product and business teams on strategy

## Required Qualifications
- Strong background in SaaS platform development
- Experience with modern web technologies (React, Node.js, Python, etc.)
- Knowledge of cloud infrastructure (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- Understanding of database design and optimization
- Experience with API design and microservices
- Leadership and mentoring abilities
- Startup/founding experience preferred

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience in construction technology or compliance software
- Background in safety or regulatory technology
- Familiarity with OSHA compliance requirements
- Experience with verification systems and credential validation
- Knowledge of B2B sales and enterprise software

## Compensation & Equity
- Competitive salary based on experience
- Significant equity founding package (to be determined)
- Benefits package including health, dental, vision
- Flexible remote work arrangements

## Hiring Process
1. Initial screening interview (30 minutes)
2. Technical deep-dive interview (60 minutes)
3. Architecture/system design discussion (60 minutes)
4. Team/culture fit interview (45 minutes)
5. Reference checks
6. Offer extension

## Success Metrics (First 90 Days)
- Technical architecture defined and approved
- Initial team hiring plan created
- Core product MVP scope defined
- Development processes and standards established
- Initial proof-of-concept or prototype demonstrated

## Reporting Structure
- Reports directly to the CEO
- Will eventually manage engineering team members
- Close collaboration with product and business leadership
