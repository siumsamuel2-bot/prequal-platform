## Data Model Update: OSHA Integration & Compliance Analytics

**What was implemented:**
- Extended `violations` table with OSHA-specific fields (citations, penalties, gravity scores, etc.)
- Added `osha_inspections` table for inspection metadata tracking
- Created `osha_api_logs` and `osha_data_freshness` tables for pipeline observability
- Designed analytics views for compliance reporting:
  - `compliance_status_by_subcontractor` (aggregate compliance metrics)
  - `osha_violation_trends` (temporal analysis of violations)
  - `expiring_certifications` (90-day expiration forecast)
  - `recent_violations` (60-day lookback)

**Schema location:** `database/schemas/03_osha_integration.sql`
